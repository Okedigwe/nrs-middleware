The mandate

Nigeria's invoices are going real-time. Is your ERP ready?

The Nigeria Revenue Service is rolling out e-invoicing in stages across taxpayer segments. Every compliant invoice now has to be structured, identified and cryptographically stamped, and that's hard to retrofit by hand.

Phased rollout

Large taxpayers first, other segments to follow

NRS has published a phased timeline with compliance dates for each taxpayer category. View the official timeline.

July 2026

Tax ID replaces TIN

The Tax ID is now the legal identifier on e-invoices, so master data has to be updated in your ERP.

Rule

Scanned invoices don't qualify

Invoices must be generated as structured data with an IRN, CSID and QR code.

From invoice to IRN in secondsNo re-keying, no portal uploads at month-end, and a complete audit trail for every document.

FAQ

Questions finance and IT leaders ask us.

What does the middleware actually do?

It sits between your ERP and the NRS e-Invoicing (MBS) platform. It receives invoices from your ERP, validates and maps them to the required structure, submits them to NRS, and writes the Invoice Reference Number (IRN), Cryptographic Stamp Identifier (CSID) and QR code back to your system.

Does a scanned PDF invoice count as an e-invoice?

No. NRS states that a paper invoice converted to electronic format by copying or scanning does not qualify. Invoices must be generated as structured data.

Can we keep using SAP?

Yes. MSL is an SAP Gold Partner and integrates SAP S/4HANA, SAP Business One and SAP Business ByDesign with the middleware, so invoices are fiscalised without changing how your team works.

Which formats are supported?

JSON, CSV and UBL XML, through the REST API or the manual upload screen.

What changed with the Tax ID?

NRS announced in July 2026 that the Tax ID replaces the TIN as the legal identifier for e-invoicing. You can retrieve yours at taxid.nrs.gov.ng. We update your customer and supplier master data as part of onboarding.

How long does onboarding take?

It depends on your ERP, invoice volumes and document types. We scope it in a short discovery session and give you a fixed plan covering mapping, sandbox testing and go-live.
