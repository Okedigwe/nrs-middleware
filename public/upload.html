Before you upload

  • Use the supplier and buyer Tax ID (it replaced the TIN in July 2026).
  • Invoices must be structured data. Scanned or photographed invoices don't qualify.
  • Line items need NRS unit-of-measure codes, VAT rate and totals.
  • Test in Sandbox mode first (Settings), then switch to live.
